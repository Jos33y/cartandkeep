# No blades, 6 October 2026

Why: eBay refused "Bosch S644D Reciprocating Sabre Saw Blades" under its UK knives policy (bladed items need age-checked delivery). Joseey: "shouldn't allow blades, knives or sharp cutting objects please". Her shop has no age check either, so both channels are blocked.

## What counts
Words on the card, draft, shop page or eBay listing: knife, knives, blade(s), razor(s), scalpel, machete, sword, dagger, axe, hatchet, cleaver, box cutter, craft/utility/Stanley knife, multi-tool, secateurs, pruning saw, loppers, scissors, shears.
Not counted: wiper, fan, propeller, rotor, blender and turbine blades; shaver, shaving, trimmer, epilator and clipper items.
Same list in src/lib/blades.js and candidate_is_bladed() in SQL.

## What changed
- golive.js: a hard check on both channels, "A blade or cutting tool: not sold on eBay or your shop. Reject it." Blocks Ready, the List buttons and bulk queueing.
- ApprovedPage: Keeper never offers to get a bladed card ready.
- 0144_no_blades.sql: candidate_is_bladed(c); amazon_source_pool and start_amazon_run skip blades, picked cards included. Run twice locally.
- check-and-cancel.sql: lists approved bladed cards and cancels their queued eBay jobs. Cards are not rejected by script; she rejects them.

## Deploy
1. SQL 0144, then check-and-cancel.sql.
2. Portal.
No edge functions changed.

## Known edges
- "Wilkinson Sword" brand matches "sword" unless "shaving" is in the name. Razors are blocked on purpose.
- A blade named only by model number with no blade word gets through; eBay refusing it shows on Alerts.
