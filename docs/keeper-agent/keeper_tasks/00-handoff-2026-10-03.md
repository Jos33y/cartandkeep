## 3. How sections work now

- **Google's taxonomy** (en-GB, 2021-09-21, 5,595 rows) is in `google_categories`. eBay's category maps to it through `google_from_ebay` (121 rows, first prefix match wins), mirrored in SQL by `google_for_listing`; the parity test keeps the two equal.
- **Sources:** `ebay`, `ai`, `checked` (studio correction, labelled "checked by hand", never shown as hers), `manual` (hers), `default` (top level only, never used for placing). Scripts never overwrite `checked` or `manual`.
- **A section** holds Google branches (`google_ids`, pruned to the top of each). A product goes to the section holding the deepest branch that contains its category. A new product with no section is placed by the trigger.
- **Filter tabs** inside a section (`google_sub`) are worked out from her products by `refresh_google_subs`. Statement triggers keep them current.
- **The sort** (`shop-sort/sort.ts`): a top-level branch over 20 products splits into its second level (Healthcare a level deeper); fewer than 3 products joins the family's largest section; a top level under 3 is left. Keeper names each section.
- **After a Keeper run** that checked anything, `keeper-run` calls `keeper_open_sections`: 3 or more uncovered products in one second-level branch open a section, marked "Opened by Keeper".
- **Merchant feed** (`google-feed`): `google_product_category` and `product_type` ("Section > tab"). **Not submitted to Merchant Center yet:** Joseph waits for more products.
- **Addresses:** a renamed section gets a new address and the old one still works (aliases kept).

## 5. Supplier cost and Amazon (built 3 October, `ck-supplier-cost`)

- **Confirmed before:** the morning read only warned ("Costs £17.98 today. You costed it at £15.28."), and a supplier's cost could not be changed after it was added.
- **Now, in the morning read (`supplier-read`, `costFollow`):** a dropship supplier's cost moves to Amazon's price when the cost came from Amazon: marked so (`cost_by 'amazon'`), empty, or equal to a price Amazon showed in this reading or the last. On a deal the cost is the price after the deal ends, never the deal price. Its postage follows when Amazon shows one. The shop product's `landed_cost` follows where it came from the supplier (empty, or noted "From the confirmed supplier" or "Amazon price").
- **Never moved:** a cost she set herself (`cost_by 'her'`), or one that matched no Amazon price (a Business or trade price). The card offers "Use £17.98 as your cost" instead, which then follows Amazon.
- **On the card:** "Change the cost" on the supplier the card prices from, and on each supplier in the list; the warning says "Your cost moved from £15.28 to £17.98 with Amazon's price on 3 Oct" (a warning for 3 days when it went up, so the Overview shows it, then a note).
- **The selling price on eBay and the shop never changes by itself.**
- **To confirm with her:** the SHAVINGFUN supplier is "Amazon Business", read from the public amazon.co.uk page. If she pays a Business price that differs from the public one, she should set it with "Change the cost" so it is hers.
