# Alerts page, current-cost warnings, price-change email

5 October 2026. `ck-alerts.zip`.

## What Joseey asked
- A full page where alerts land, since the Overview's Amazon panel ran to the bottom of the page.
- Self-clearing: nothing to tick off. An alert is there while its cause is, gone once fixed.
- SHAVINGFUN still said "Amazon: now £17.98" after the cost was set to £17.98.
- Only price-change alerts reach her by email, to support@cartandkeep.com.

## SHAVINGFUN, the cause
- The morning read saves the cost it compared against (`supplier_read.costed`). The warning compared Amazon's price with that copy, not with her cost now, so it stayed until the next read.
- `costedNow(sources, read)` in `src/lib/supplierWatch.js` takes the cost off the supplier the reading belongs to (`source_id`, else the same ASIN). `warningsOf(read, sources)` is what Approved, the Alerts page and the Overview use.
- "Up to £X" drops to a note when her cost already covers it.
- Dispatch over two weeks ("6 to 7 months", Croydex) is red, not amber.

## Alerts page (`/alerts`, in the sidebar under Overview)
Worked out on every read (`src/lib/alerts.js`, `useAlerts`), on opening, on returning to the tab, and every five minutes.

| Alert | Clears when |
|---|---|
| Live listing's Amazon supplier: out of stock, gone, long dispatch, dearer than her cost, cost moved, not sent by Amazon | the next read says otherwise, she changes the cost, or (cost moved) she changes the price |
| eBay queue failed to list a card, last 14 days | it lists, or it is queued again |
| Keeper run paused, with the reason | resumed or stopped |
| Low stock on her shop, 3 or fewer | restocked |
| Approved cards with supplier warnings, one line | the warnings clear |

- Change price opens Listings at that listing with Change price showing (`/listings?find=<item>&price=1`).
- The approved line opens Approved with the new "Supplier warnings" chip.
- Sidebar badge: red and amber on what is selling. Approved warnings are left out of it.
- Overview: two columns. Left: Needs you, eBay 90 days, Rejected. Right: Alerts (worst 3, See all), Money. The Amazon suppliers panel and `useSupplierWatch` are gone.

## Price-change email
- `supplier-read`, after saving the readings, emails through `notify` (kind `price_alert`) to the shop's own address (`tenants.store.email`, support@cartandkeep.com).
- Only cards live on eBay. Two cases (`supplier-read/alert.ts`):
  - moved: her cost followed Amazon, and the landed cost changed.
  - dearer: Amazon over a cost she set herself. Told once per new price (`supplier_read.told`).
- One email per batch, each line with old and new cost, the eBay price, and a Change price link.
- A send that fails is logged, not retried, as with every email here.
