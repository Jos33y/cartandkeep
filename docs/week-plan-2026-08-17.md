tasks, and an SEO task cannot be executed against a site that does not exist.
So the sequence is storefront live, then SEO items, then the report.

```

SEO items executed   Thu 20 to Fri 21
month one verified   Sat 22
SEO report           Mon 24, reporting on work done Thu and Fri
```

Wednesday is the real deadline. Saturday is the visible one.

## Risks, and what to do when they land

**The storefront is not live Wednesday.** Then the SEO items compress into
Friday and the 24 August report is thin. Mitigation is to cut catalogue depth
before cutting the launch date. A store with 20 products live on Wednesday
beats a store with 97 products live on Friday.

**She reviews the batches and rejects most of them.** Expected, and the
rejection reasons are live so it is the first real feedback the pipeline has
had. It is data, not a setback. Do not respond to it this week beyond thanking
her for it.

**Something breaks in the import on Friday.** Backups exist and the diff works.
Take one before, take one after, run `--against`.

**You run out of week.** Cut in this order: catalogue depth, then store polish,
then the Friday batch prep, then the SEO report format. Never cut the
storefront being live and never cut an update.
