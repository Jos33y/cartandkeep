How long does delivery actually take?
−
Two to four working days to most of the UK. Orders placed before 1pm go out the same day, Monday to Friday. The Highlands, the islands and Northern Ireland take a day or two longer, and you will see the honest estimate on the product page before you add anything to the basket.

Do you deliver to the Scottish Highlands and islands?
−
Yes, everywhere in the UK, at the same price. Some heavier items take four to six days to reach IV, KW, PA, PH, HS, ZE and BT postcodes. Nothing is surcharged and nothing is excluded.

Terms of sale

The agreement between you and the shop when you buy something. Written to be read, but it is still a contract, so the wording is deliberately exact.
Nothing here removes your statutory rights
Governed by the law of England and Wales
1
Who we are
Cart & Keep is a trading name of a company registered in England and Wales. Our registered office and company number appear in the footer of every page. When these terms say we, us or the shop, they mean that company.
2
These terms
These are the terms on which we sell to you. Please read them before you order. The version that applies to your order is the one published on the day you placed it, and the version number and date sit at the top of this page.
2.1
We may change these terms. Changes are not retrospective.
2.2
Every past version stays available, so you can always read the one you agreed to.
3
Placing an order
Your order is an offer to buy. The contract begins when we email you to confirm it. If we cannot supply something, we will tell you and refund in full. You do not need an account to order, and we do not create one for you.
4
Price and payment
linked from checkout
The price shown is the price you pay. It includes VAT where VAT applies. We do not show a struck-through was-price, we do not run countdown timers, and we do not raise a price in order to discount it later.
4.1
Delivery is £4.95, or free on baskets over £40.
4.2
Stripe takes the payment. We never see or store your card details.
4.3
If a price is obviously wrong we will contact you before charging anything.
5
Delivery
We deliver anywhere in the UK at one price. Estimates are given in working days and shown before you pay. Risk passes to you on delivery, ownership on payment.
Practical questions about delivery are answered in
Help · Delivery, 9 answers
6
Product descriptions
Some product text is written by whoever supplied the item rather than by us, and it is labelled as theirs on the product page. Where a supplier claim and a legal requirement disagree, the legal requirement wins. Photographs are illustrative; sizes and quantities in the specification table are the ones that count.
7
Your right to cancel
Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you may cancel within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. We give you 30 days, which is longer than the law requires, and nothing in this clause reduces your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

How it diverges from help
No search, no accordion, nothing collapsed. Everything is visible because a contract you cannot read in full is not a contract. Numbered clauses and sub-clauses so it can be cited: clause 4.2 is a thing you can point at.
A rail, not a floating card
The contents run the full height of the document in their own tinted column, which is what fixes the 500px empty gutter on the current page. It also carries the version block, so provenance sits beside the text rather than under it.
Versioned, not "last reviewed"
v3, superseding v2, with a diff and a PDF. A reviewed date tells a reader nothing. A version they can retrieve is the difference between a page and a record.
Cross-linked both ways
Clause 5 points at the nine delivery answers. The returns answer points back at clause 7. Help handles "how do I", terms handle "what am I owed", and neither pretends to be the other.
The decision underneath both
Five pages, two templates, not one.
Delivery and Returns move into the help centre as groups, where the questions already are. Terms, Privacy, Cookies and Accessibility stay in the reference set with the rail and the version block. That is one fewer template than exists today and it stops a contract and a shop assistant sharing a layout.
Open, needs your call
One icon family, chosen once: Phosphor or Lucide. Everything I have built uses type and rules only, which is honest but thin. Search, chevrons, the basket and the delivery states all want a real glyph.
