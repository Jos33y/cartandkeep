02

Department colour

Eight hues, one lightness, one chroma. Wayfinding, not decoration.

Every hue sits at oklch(0.55 0.11 H) so they read as one family rather than eight decisions. The discipline that keeps the accent working: department colour only ever appears as a filled field, a tab or a rule. It never lands on a control. Terracotta still means act and nothing else. Gold still means reduced and nothing else.

Two up, not one
A single column at 375 turns 78 products into an endless scroll. Two up with the price at 19px still reads at arm's length.
The delivery bar earns its place
A persistent strip showing the gap to the £40 threshold. It is the single highest-value piece of motion in the shop, because it moves every time something goes in the basket, and it lifts basket value honestly rather than by pressure.
Colour does the wayfinding
On a phone there is no breadcrumb worth reading. The department field at the top and the tab on every card tell you where you are without a word.
06

Six pieces of motion

All of it on tokens you already declared and never used
Add to basket
dur-settle
Optimistic. The count changes before the request returns, the item rises into the header, the delivery bar fills. Reverts with an apology if it fails.
Card to product
view-transition
The product image persists from the grid into the PDP instead of the page blinking. Native, cheap, and it will still look right in 2032.
Free delivery bar
dur-settle
Fills toward £40 and lands on a plain sentence when it arrives. The only persuasion the shop needs and the only one it has promised to use.
Card hover
dur 120ms
Two pixels of lift, the shadow deepens one step, quick view fades in. Currently nothing happens at all, which is why the grid feels dead.
Skeletons, not pop-in
dur-settle
Filter and sort redraw the grid in place at the same card dimensions. Nothing jumps, nothing reflows.
Search as you type
dur 120ms
Results narrow live, on the PLP and in the help centre. At 1000 products this stops being a nicety and becomes the shop.
Next, once you pick 1a or 1b
Two hero directions, then the PDP, then the help centre.
The hero is the loudest failure so it goes next, and it will not depend on photography you do not have. The PDP needs the attributes pulled out of the feed rather than a design pass. The help centre is a different template from the legal pages, as you said.
