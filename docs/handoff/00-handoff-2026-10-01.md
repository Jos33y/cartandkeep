## The publish error, confirmed and inferred

eBay said: "Input error. Seller Inventory Service can not publish the data. Product not found."

| | Finding |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | Headers are right on every call: `Content-Language` and `Accept-Language` en-GB, `X-EBAY-C-MARKETPLACE-ID` EBAY_GB. |
| Confirmed | The inventory item PUT and the offer both succeed. Only the publish call is refused. |
| Confirmed | The barcode Amazon gives for SHAVINGFUN is `734730596013`: 12 digits, a UPC. We sent every barcode as `ean`. |
| Inferred | eBay looked up 734730596013 as an EAN at publish, found nothing, and refused. This is the most likely cause, not a proven one. |

**The fix in `_shared/ebay/listing.ts`:**
- `barcodeFields(gtin)`: 12 digits are sent as `upc`, 8 or 13 digits as `ean`, and 14 digits are left off (eBay's inventory item has no field for them).
- `itemBody(p, epid, bare)` builds the inventory item.
- `publish()` catches error 25604, or the words "Product not found", once:
  - it puts the item up again with no barcode, no ePID, and no EAN, UPC, ISBN or GTIN item details, then publishes again;
  - it records `barcode_dropped` on the card;
  - any other refusal goes through unchanged, and is never retried.
- `EbayError` now carries eBay's `errorId`s.
- Tests: `listing_test.ts`, 79 pass in the shared folder.

**eBay errors met so far, so they are not met again:**

| Date | Error | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 Sep | 216305 | MAILING_BOX package type refused on the UK site | No package type is sent |
| 1 Oct | 25604, Product not found | Most likely the barcode in the wrong field | Above |

Before adding any new field to the inventory item or the offer, read eBay's Inventory API page for that field and the UK site's rules.

## Things already answered, so they are not asked again

- **Weight:** never blocks a publish unless her eBay postage policy is calculated by weight. Hers is flat or free.
- **Price above the market:** fine. "Let it list successfully, that's what matters."
- **Amazon reading:** every morning at 7am London time, plus on demand. A reading under 6 hours old is reused (`FRESH_HOURS = 6`).
- **The Send to Cart & Keep bookmark:** sits under the supplier box on the card.
- **Keepr moving a card:** it may move a card to an eBay listing on its own at 95% sure or more. Below that it only suggests.
- **Model:** `claude-sonnet-5-5` everywhere.
