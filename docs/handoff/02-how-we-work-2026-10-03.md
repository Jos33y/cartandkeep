## House rules (Joseph's words, all in force)

- British English. No em or en dashes anywhere, code included. No AI-tell words. No emojis.
- Customer copy says "you". Portal copy never says "she" or "her": it says "you", or states the fact.
- Her words are never edited: "Shop it! Love it! Keep it!", "A small UK shop with a very large cupboard".
- No street address outside the legal pages. No dev notes on public pages.
- Secrets never in files, zips, collectors or memory.
- Scripts never touch her work in the portal: her decisions, notes, sources, or figures on cards she has decided.
- Premium and calm, never lazy or generated-looking. Compact over big: a strip of pills, not a band of tiles.
- Model for the platform: `claude-sonnet-5-5`.

## How to work with Joseph

- **"Don't code" means answer only.** Build only on "build", "yes" or "go". A remark is an idea, not an order.
- **Dissect before designing.** When he says "lets dissect" or "let me explain", stop, listen, and ask one or two focused questions. Do not design ahead of his model of the business.
- **Answer what was asked.** A screenshot with no question is context. "Why?" wants the working, not a new plan.
- **Push back once, with evidence, then do it his way.**
- **Short confirmations close an item** ("all done", "sql ran"). Move on.
- **He tests live** with her real data and sends screenshots, query results and pasted errors. Read every one closely.
- **Act like a human seller, not a compliance system.** Before adding any check, ask whether eBay itself would stop it. If not, it is a note, not a blocker.

## Working method

- **Never guess file contents.** The workspace starts empty. Ask for a collector or the file. A copy from an earlier collector may be stale: say so.
- **Collectors:** Node scripts in `scripts/` (`collect-ck-<what>.mjs`), run from the repo root, writing `ck-collect-<what>-<date>.zip` with a MANIFEST of what was found and missing, never `.env`. The older PowerShell ones still exist. Make each as tight as the work.
- **Every diagnosis marks what is confirmed and what is inferred,** and says how to find out when unsure (a read-only query, the Network tab, an `explain`).
- **Before changing an eBay call,** read eBay's documentation for every field sent, and handle known refusals in code.
- **Trace data end to end:** if something is fetched, prove it reaches the card, the draft, the shop and eBay.
- **Test by using it:** type real values, click, use the keys, check all 13 sizes (320, 360, 375, 390, 414, 600, 768, 820, 1024, 1280, 1341, 1440, 1920), run the build and the tests. Fix what you find before handing over.
- **SQL runs against real Postgres before shipping,** twice for a migration. Logic that mirrors the database (filters, counts, sorts) is checked against Postgres on generated rows.
- **Deliver a zip of only the changed files,** `ck-<what>-<date>.zip`, with a MANIFEST in this order: **SQL first, then functions, then the portal.** A portal change that reads a new column breaks its page if the SQL is not in yet (Approved went blank on 3 October).
- **Name every function to deploy,** including any other function that imports a changed shared file (check with `Select-String -Path supabase\functions\*\index.ts -Pattern "<file>.ts"`).
- **Project docs** are updated when a stretch of work ends: the handoff, the task brief, the scripts reference.

## Product decisions to date

**Listing**
- Only eBay's own refusals block a listing. Price, weight and photo concerns inform.
- Any photo may go up except another eBay seller's own `i.ebayimg.com` photo. The first photo should be a plain shot.
- Keeper moves a card to another eBay listing on its own at 95% sure or more; below that it suggests.

**Keeper**
- Reads only when a check is started from Products or the office. Nothing reads a draft on arrival or on opening.
- Decides like a seller: returns, label warnings it can read, title, address, old questions. Never puts a product on sale, never changes what she saved, never touches what is on sale.
- Runs in batches she sizes (20, 50, 100, all), with a spending limit. About 1.7p a draft.
- **New, 3 October:** Keeper approves a candidate when it can be sourced perfectly on Amazon (task 02). It still never rejects, prices, publishes or adds to the shop.

**Sections and categories**
- Google's product taxonomy decides the sections, for ranking and Merchant. The tree is worked out in code; Keeper only names sections.
- A studio correction is `checked`, never shown as her choice (`manual`).

**Suppliers and cost**
- Her first source is Amazon, on her **Prime** account. Delivery to her is free; she repackages and posts to the buyer herself. Not VAT registered, so the public price with VAT is her cost.
- Her cost per unit is the Amazon price only; postage to the buyer and packing are counted per sale.
- A cost that came from Amazon follows Amazon's price every morning; a deal price is never a cost. A cost she sets herself is never moved.
- The selling price never changes by itself.

**Pages**
- Listings means live: active on eBay or on sale on the shop. Drafts live in Products.
- Products has search (title, brand, SKU, barcode, item number) and four pills under the title that open their tabs.

**Later, by his word**
- Sitemap and IndexNow: backlog.
- Keeper's titles to eBay: after 200 products have gone from the shop to eBay.
- Merchant feed: submit once there are enough products.
