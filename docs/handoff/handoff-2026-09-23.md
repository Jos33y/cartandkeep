## The pipeline, as it runs today

```
shelf-hb.js        collect a shop            no eBay calls
shelf-queries.js   draft queries             no eBay calls
scan.js            1 call a phrase           refuses crowded, cheap, dead
gate.js            7 to 21 calls a query     refuses on sales, entry, price
import-gate.js     writes the batch          no eBay calls
```

Both the scan and the gate re-judge from what they already measured, so moving
a threshold costs nothing:

```
node scripts/scan.js --from-csv=research/scans/<f>.csv --csv=<same> --out=<queries>
node scripts/gate.js --from-snapshot=.gate/<f>.json --band=<band> --csv=<same> --out=<queries>
```

The gate needs `--band` passed, because a row does not carry its own.

### The rules in force

| Test | Refuses when |
|---|---|
| Crowding | over 250 live listings |
| Entry | no small seller converting, or one barely selling, or untested in a crowded market |
| Market size | under 10 units a month across the sample |
| Goods window | the ceiling is under £3 or over £40 |
| Band | priced over £80 on the default Evri band, so it is not a parcel |
| Cost base | the market price cannot carry the fee stack |
| Target | under £2 a sale at the list price or at the converting price |

Entry proved became a pass condition today. It is the single change that
stopped markets she cannot rank on reaching her.
