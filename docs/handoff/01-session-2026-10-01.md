## 1 October, mid-morning: Listings rebuilt

- **Collector first:** `collect-ck-listings.ps1`, then her real counts.
- **Listings shows only what is live:**
  - active eBay listings from the latest pull, portal publishes since that pull, and shop products on sale;
  - no drafts, no queue, nothing ended.
- **Migrations:**
  - `0119`: the eBay quantity left, and the item number on sales;
  - `0120`: the `live_listings` view and `live_listing_counts`, with indexes;
  - `0121`: shop insight from `store_events`;
  - `0122`: her averages.
- **Speed:** 7 seconds came down to about 90ms on 6,000 test listings, by splitting one OR lookup into two indexed ones.
- **The page:**
  - a table read 60 rows at a time, sorted and filtered in the database;
  - a sticky toolbar with tabs (All, On both, eBay only, Shop only) and filters (Selling, Not on your shop, Out, Low, Quiet, Wanted);
  - the shop mark in words ("12 views", "no views yet", "3 baskets, no sale");
  - an Opened column with "1 in N bought", green when above her average.
- **The opened row:**
  - the eBay panel shows rates against her own average, and Update on eBay;
  - the shop panel is one of three: a funnel when on sale; the gaps with links to the exact step when a draft, plus Put on sale; or Add to your shop when not there.
- Zips: `ck-listings-live`, `ck-listings-table`, `ck-listings-insight`, `ck-listings-v3` (each replaces the one before), `collect-ck-insights.ps1`.
- **Verified with her data:**
  - 452 live: 22 on both, 430 eBay only;
  - 192 shop drafts, 238 not on the shop;
  - out 26, low 82, quiet 35, wanted 4, selling 22;
  - average 1 in 99 opens bought, open rate 1.6%.
