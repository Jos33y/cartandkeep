# Store: trade routes for the top best-selling brands

29 September 2026 · internal · web research, every line from the page linked

Brands ranked by how many Google UK best sellers (Home & Garden, Aug 2026, Nov and Dec 2025) are seen in a UK shop: `research/picks/brands.csv`. This file says how a small new UK online shop can buy each at trade price.

## Open to a new small online shop, conditions published

| Brand | Route | Conditions on the page |
|---|---|---|
| VonShef (also VonHaus) | DOMU Brands, direct trade account. https://www.vonhaus.com/vonhaus-trade-enquiries | £500 minimum order; basic company details, approval in 1 to 3 working days; e-commerce sellers named as customers; no active sales on Amazon, eBay and similar; images only those DOMU publishes or approves |
| Joseph Joseph | Direct sales enquiry. https://www.josephjoseph.com/pages/trade-portal | Digital assets download and product data through NuOrder. Minimum order not stated |

| Premier Decorations | Direct B2B registration, "start trading within 24 hours". https://www.premierdec.com/trade/new_trader.php | Not stated |
| CareCo | Professionals account, retailers named. https://www.careco.co.uk/professionals | Discount off retail, more on bulk. Also graded ex-demo stock to trade@careco.co.uk |
| Silentnight, Sealy, Snug bedding | eBedding Trade, B2B wholesaler that names online retailers as customers. https://trade.e-bedding.co.uk/about-us/ | Register to see prices; free delivery over £100 |
