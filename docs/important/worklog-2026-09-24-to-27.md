## 1. How we work

### The pipeline, in order

1. **Collect a shelf.** A shop's product list, read from its Shopify feed, its product pages, or only its sitemap. No eBay calls.
2. **Draft queries.** One eBay search phrase per product. No eBay calls.
3. **Scan.** One eBay call per phrase. Throws out markets that are crowded, empty, too narrow, too dear, or account risk. The survivors go to the gate.
4. **Gate.** About 7 calls per phrase at `--sample=6`. Reads real listings: who is selling, how fast, at what price, and whether a seller her size is getting in.
5. **Import.** Passes and set-asides become cards in her portal, in a tab (Care & DIY, General, H & B).

### The rules of the routine

- **5,000 eBay calls a day, reset 08:00 UK.** Check with `node scripts/limits.js` before each new scan.
- **Never scan more than can be gated the same day.** A scan sends roughly half its phrases to the gate on care files, less on trade and general. Budget about 450 calls to scan a file, plus 7 per phrase it sends on.
- **Scan ahead at night.** Spare calls in the evening go on scanning the next day's first slices. A scan stays valid for a day, so the next morning starts on gates.
- **Rotate the sources.** Every source gets a slice a day (about 250 phrases), so none sits untouched and each shows how it pays.
- **Check the ledger between files.** `node scripts/ledger.mjs` shows what is scanned, part done and untouched, and where to resume.
- **Paste every scan summary, upload every gate snapshot.** Wrong products are caught before they reach her (the sling that matched a backpack, the underwear that matched incontinence pants, the tester pot against a 2.5 litre tin).
- **Rule changes are re-judged, not re-scanned.** A saved scan or snapshot can be judged again under new rules at no cost.
- **Her work is never touched.** Imports, removals and decisions leave any card she has decided, noted or sourced exactly as it is.
- **Import date is the day imported.** One batch per day and term; the tab name (label) gathers all of them.

### Drafting (no eBay calls)

`scripts/shelf-queries.js`

```powershell
node scripts/shelf-queries.js --csv=research/shelf/<shelf>.csv[,<shelf2>.csv] [--done-map=<scan or map csv>] [--with-out-of-stock] --out=research/queries/queries-<source>
```

Writes `queries-<source>-evri5-N.txt` (450 phrases each), `-evri15-N`, `-letter500-N`, and a map `queries-<source>-map.csv`. `--done-map` skips phrases already scanned. `--with-out-of-stock` keeps lines listed ahead of their stock (Lakeland's Christmas range).

What it does to each product name, as of 27 September:

- One query per product: model number, then brand plus product, then the product alone.
- A shop's own name in the brand field is no brand ("Essential Aids", "Complete Care Shop").
- For names read from a sitemap, the brand is taken from the start of the name ("joseph joseph …").
- Trade case counts dropped from codes ("0025378x12" becomes "0025378"); region suffixes dropped ("-GB").
- Accents folded ("cafetière" becomes "cafetiere"); stray single letters dropped.
- Extras after "with", "w/", a dash or a comma are cut; colours dropped.
- Sized clothing and footwear cut; aids never cut for size.
- Order: shelf price £3 to £45 first, dearest first; then names with no price; then the rest.

Subsets by hand, for a focused slice (example, Lakeland parts):

```powershell
Import-Csv research\queries\queries-lakeland-map.csv | Where-Object { ($_.name -match '\brefills?\b|\breplacement\b|\bspares?\b|\bcartridges?\b' -or $_.query -match '[a-z]+\d{2,}|\d{4,}') -and $_.band -eq 'evri5' } | ForEach-Object { "{0,-38}# {1}" -f $_.query, $_.name } | Set-Content research\queries\queries-lakeland-parts.txt
```

### eBay (calls)

| Script | Calls | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `scripts/limits.js` | none | `node scripts/limits.js` |
| `scripts/scan.js` | 1 per phrase | `node scripts/scan.js --file=research/queries/<file>.txt [--start=N] [--max=250] --csv=research/scans/scan-<name>.csv --out=research/queries/gated-<name>.txt --band=evri5 --max-listings=600 --min-results=5` |
| `scripts/scan.js --from-csv` | none | re-judge a saved scan under today's rules: `--from-csv=<scan csv> --csv=<same> --out=<gated file>` plus the same flags |
| `scripts/gate.js` | about 7 per phrase | `node scripts/gate.js --file=research/queries/gated-<name>.txt --band=evri5 --sample=6 --csv=research/gates/gate-<name>.csv --out=research/queries/passes-<name>.txt` |
| `scripts/gate.js --from-snapshot` | none | re-judge a saved gate: `--from-snapshot=.gate\<file>.json --band=evri5 --csv=<gate csv> --out=<passes>` |

Scan details: UK-located sellers only; `--min-results=5` lets small specific markets through. `--start` and `--max` count **lines in the file**, the same way the ledger does, so the ledger's resume number is always the one to use. Account risk is held back after the slice is taken, by the rules in `scripts/lib/risk.js`: second hand, sized clothing, counterfeit-prone brands, fragrance (by word, and by house when sold by the ml or with no shop name), alcohol, gas canisters, nicotine, pharmacy, slimming and hair-regrowth medicines, and calendars, diaries or planners dated before the current year.

Gate details: Ctrl+C **once** saves what is done and writes the rest to `<file>-rest.txt`; a 429 does the same. Early sales (3 or more in under a week) prove entry; small markets with a small seller proved are set aside, not refused; under £2 is set aside with its figure.

### Keeping track

`scripts/ledger.mjs` (the one that updates itself)

```powershell
node scripts/ledger.mjs
```

Reads every query file in `research/queries` and checks each line against every scan CSV in `research/scans` and every gate CSV in `research/gates`. A line counts as dealt with if a scan read it, or if the scan holds it back under the same rules (`scripts/lib/risk.js`). The resume point is the first line that is neither, so it is exact. Prints the files that are started, and writes the full table to `research/ledger.md`:

- **scanned**: every line read or held back
- **part, next --start=N**: little after line N has been read; resume there with `--start=N`
- **gaps: N unchecked lines in left/<file>**: most of the file was read, but some lines in the middle never were (older scans skipped words we have since stopped skipping: cards, games, books, stationery, instruments). Those lines alone are written to `research/queries/left/<file>`; scan that file whole, and nothing is repeated
- **untouched**

Nothing is kept by hand, so it cannot fall behind. Replaced drafts and one-off lists live in `research/queries/old/` and are not counted.
