**What Is CBD Dropshipping?**
-----------------------------

!How CBD dropshipping works

First and foremost, let’s clarify what CBD actually is. Cannabidiol (CBD) is an **active ingredient in cannabis** that comes from the hemp plant, but it **does not cause a high** and is **not addictive** when it’s under 0.3% THC (tetrahydrocannabinol). Many people use it for anxiety, insomnia, chronic pain, seizures, and many other **medical purposes.**

Now, **CBD dropshipping** is the process of selling products containing hemp-derived cannabidiol through an online store. The difference between a traditional CBD company and dropshipping CBD is that **you don’t need to hold inventory**. You work with a third-party dropshipping supplier who packs and fulfills you customer’s orders when placed through your store.

Is it profitable to dropship CBD? Absolutely. The CBD marketing was estimated at **$10.68 billion** by the end of 2025, and is projected to reach **$30.96 billion** by 2033, according to **GrandView Research**. 

**Is It Legal To Dropship CBD?**
--------------------------------

!Legality of CBD dropshipping

Let’s go straight to the main question: **dropshipping CBD products is 100% legal.** That said, these are regulated in dropshipping, so there are a few little considerations to keep in mind. For instance, all 50 states have laws legalizing CBD with different degrees of restrictions. 

Either way, with **hemp being made legal in the USA,** it made it virtually impossible to keep CBD illegal. That would be like making oranges legal, but keeping orange juice illegal. 

But here’s the thing: CBD is legal as long as it comes from hemp, but not if it comes from cannabis (marijuana). In fact, many people obtain CBD online without a medical marijuana license, which is legal in most states.

So, here are the basics of dropshipping CBD products:

*   Make sure to offer **hemp-derived products only.** 
*   **Look out for THC limits.** Legal CBD products in the US must contain 0.3% or less Delta-9 THC. In the UK and many parts of Europe, the limit is often lower, typically 0.2% THC.
*   Make sure your products include **lab testing (COA)**. Partner with suppliers that offer a Certificate of Analysis (COA) for every batch. This ensures your products are meeting all legal compliance and THC limits, and don’t include extra contaminants.

**Things You Need To Take Care Of When Dropshipping CBD Products**
------------------------------------------------------------------

CBD dropshipping is totally legal. But, like any other niche, it comes with its own considerations and best practices. Moreover, CBD products are related to health, so they require a bit more certification and double-checking.

First, **set up your store as a real business.** This means registering your business and getting the necessary licenses to operate legally. This is the basis for a fully compliant, legal, and transparent dropshipping store.

Next, take time to **understand the legal landscape.** As we’ve mentioned, CBD regulations vary by country and by state. This impacts THC limits, labeling, and what products can actually be sold or shipped to certain locations. On top of that, there’s intellectual property and branding. All in all, avoid selling products that misuse trademarks or copy existing brands.

Lastly, make sure to work with **compliant, well-documented products.** Look for suppliers that provide lab testing (COAs), clear ingredient lists, and proper labeling. This will absolutely keep you out of trouble and make your brand more trustworthy.
