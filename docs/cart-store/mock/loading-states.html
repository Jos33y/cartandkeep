The app boot

Shown once, while the shop chunk arrives. The basket strokes on, the ampersand lands. It replaces a grey "Loading" that told a first time visitor nothing about whose shop they had opened. The portal and the status page keep the plain one.

Opening the shop

The catalogue, loading

Exact geometry of what is coming: same square, same padding, same two title lines, same price height, same gutters, same breakpoints. Nothing moves when the products land. The sweep is warm to a lighter warm at 100 degrees, so it reads as light crossing a surface rather than a bar sliding along a track. No white band, because a white band on a cream page is the tell.

Motion off

With prefers-reduced-motion the sweep stops and the blocks stay, because the layout they hold is the point and the animation never was. The mark holds its finished state rather than looping faster. Turn it on in your OS settings and reload to check.
