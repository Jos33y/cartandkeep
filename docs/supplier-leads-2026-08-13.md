# Supplier leads, mobility and daily living aids

**13 August 2026.** Found by web search in twenty minutes. Nothing here is
confirmed. Every price and term below needs a trade account or a phone call,
and until one exists these are leads rather than sources.

Recorded because the last session proved the order: search first, build an
adapter later or never. Two days of CJ work produced one freight figure. Ten
minutes of searching produced four named UK suppliers with real prices.

---

## Why this is worth doing now, when it was closed before

The instruction after the pet batch was no further supplier **API** work
without her asking. That stands. This is not API work.

Two things also changed.

**The category is different.** Pet bedding was UK mill product with no
dropship route, which is why it carried headroom nobody could reach. Mobility
aids are distributed generics: the half step in this batch shows 20 to 31
sellers on the same SKU, and the grab hand rail shows 41. That many sellers on
one product means a supplier is holding it and selling to all of them.

**The CJ account needs a transaction by roughly 24 August** or access goes.

## The lead that matters

**Mobility2You** at `mobility2you.trade`

A UK wholesale supplier of disability aids and incontinence products, selling
to pharmacies and NHS prescribers rather than to the public. Their catalogue
matches this batch directly rather than approximately.

What they list that we already measured:

| They stock | In our batch |
|---|---|
| Senator Raised Toilet Seat, 50mm and 100mm | raised toilet seat cluster, 22 products |
| Viscount Raised Toilet Seat | appeared in our own scrape at £16.84 |
| Raised toilet seat and frame, flatpack | toilet frame cluster |
| Aidapt Male Urinal | urinal rows, screened out on category |
| Grab rails, assorted, discounted | grab rail cluster, 54 products |
| R8 and Ovi-Lite rollators | walkers and canes cluster |
| Walking stick selection, mixed packs | walking stick rows |

That is four of our seven clusters from one supplier.

**Why this shape of supplier is the right one.** They sell to pharmacies, so
their customers are small businesses buying a few units rather than pallets.
That usually means a workable minimum order. They are UK based, so there is no
import VAT she cannot reclaim, no customs delay, and no fifty day transit
against eBay's delivery expectations. And they are a wholesaler rather than a
retailer, which matters because eBay permits fulfilling from a wholesaler and
not from another retailer.

**What is unknown and decides everything.** Trade prices are behind an account.
Minimum order is not published. Whether they dropship to an end customer at all
is not stated. Those three answers turn this from a lead into a source.

## Also found, not yet looked at

**Drive DeVilbiss** manufacture commodes in the UK at their Welland facility,
and Mobility2You resell them. Buying direct is a different conversation and a
larger one.

**MX Wholesale** and **TB Trade** both run UK dropship programmes with no
minimum order and same day dispatch. General catalogues rather than mobility
specialists, so the question is whether they carry any of this at all.

**Avasam** remains the parked option from `supplier-apis.md`. UK stock, verified
suppliers, and an API on some tier. Worth a look only after a manual route is
proven, on the same principle as above.
