Most home manicures chip early for two reasons: oil left on the nail, and coats that are too thick. Fix those two and polish lasts noticeably longer, whatever brand you use. Quick dry polish helps too, because it sets the surface fast, but the layers underneath still need time to harden.

## Why does nail polish chip so quickly?

- **Oil on the nail.** Hand cream and natural oils stop polish gripping.
- **Thick coats.** A thick coat stays soft underneath for longer and dents easily.
- **Unsealed tips.** The free edge of the nail takes the most knocks, and chips start there.
- **Hot water too soon.** Washing up or a hot shower straight after painting softens fresh polish.

## What is the best routine for a manicure that lasts?

1. **Shape and clean.** File in one direction, then wipe each nail with a little remover to take off any oil.
2. **Base coat.** One thin layer. It helps polish grip and stops dark colours staining the nail.
3. **Two thin coats of colour.** Thin coats dry harder and faster than one thick one.
4. **Cap the tip.** Run the brush lightly along the free edge of each nail to seal it.
5. **Top coat.** It adds shine and protects the colour. Add another thin top coat every couple of days to keep it fresh.
6. **Wait before hot water.** Give it time before washing up, a bath or anything that bumps your nails.

## What does quick dry nail polish actually do?

Quick dry polish is made to set on the surface fast, so it stops being tacky and smudges less easily within a short time. That is useful when you are painting your nails between other things.

What it does not do is harden all the way through at the same speed. Treat your nails gently for a while after painting, even when they feel dry to the touch.

## Why does nail polish bubble?

Usually because of air in the polish or coats that are too thick. Roll the bottle gently between your palms rather than shaking it, and keep each coat thin.

## How should you store nail polish?

Keep bottles upright, closed tightly, and somewhere cool and out of direct sunlight. Wipe the neck of the bottle before closing it so the cap seals properly and the polish does not thicken.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does nail polish take to dry fully?

It depends on the polish and how thick the coats are. The surface can feel dry quickly, especially with quick dry formulas, but the layers underneath take longer to harden. Thin coats dry fastest.

### Do I really need a base coat?

It is not essential, but it helps polish grip and last, and it stops strong colours staining your nails.

### Why does my polish peel off in one piece?

That is usually oil or moisture on the nail before painting. Wipe each nail with remover first and let it dry.

### Does cold water make nail polish dry faster?

Cool air or water can help the surface set, but it does not harden the layers underneath. Thin coats and a good top coat make more difference.
