ck-listings-once-2026-10-03
Portal only. No SQL. No functions to deploy.

Listings reads every live listing once when the page opens (and on reload), then sorts, filters, searches and counts
in the browser. A tab, filter, sort or search no longer asks the database anything. A read still going when a new one
starts is cancelled.
