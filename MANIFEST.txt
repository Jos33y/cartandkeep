The pack rule fixed, and the cases that would come later (Joseey, 3 October: "go for all of it").

What changed in Keeper's look on Amazon:
- A count rules a card out only when Amazon names a real multipack ("Pack of 3", "9 Pack", "Twin Pack", "Multipack",
  "250ml x 12", "2 x 2L"), or both titles state a count and they differ (Dentastix 28 against 7). Short card titles
  ("Lego 77260") are no longer read as "a single".
- What is inside one product is never a pack: 292 Pieces, 108 Pcs, 75 Tablets, 28 Sticks; "N x size" after kit, set,
  with, includes ("Kit (3 x 450ml spray)", "with 2 x 500ml Refills").
- Renewed, refurbished or used listings are ruled out: she sells new.
- Approved with a warning for Approved, shown on the card ("Amazon: check the pack" / "Amazon: a bundle"):
  Amazon's "Number of Items" over one; a bundle ("+ Free Spoon"); Amazon at 2.5 times or more the research's price;
  a card that is a pack where Amazon's page names none.
- More than one result: another colour, size or weight is another variant and is not picked.
- No seller on the page says "no seller shown", not "another seller".
36 tests pass, with every title from the runs and the likely next ones.
